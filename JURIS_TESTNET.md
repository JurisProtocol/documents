### Connect To Testnet

Navigate to https://wallet.terra-classic.io/. Then in the top drop down menu choose **Terra Classic Tesnet**. Then on the top right connect your Keplr wallet. In the navigation pane then click on **My Wallet** to show your balances. You can click on **Manage Tokens** to add the Juris Token deployment on Testnet (see below for contract address).
